current status

Fresh out of school, building things.

I'm Kitty — a data analyst, privacy advocate, and chaos engineer based in the midwest. I just finished my BS in Data Analytics and I'm in that strange, electric in-between: not a student anymore, not yet wherever I'm going next.

Data should serve people, not exploit them.

I'm spending this time building things that matter to me — a portfolio, a home lab, educational resources for women entering tech — and figuring out what kind of work I actually want to do in the world.

what i'm working on

Chaos Gremlin HQ.

My portfolio site is an ongoing experiment — part professional showcase, part digital laboratory. Current projects include a manufacturing defect analysis, an employee attrition ML pipeline, and a Beginner's Guide to Pandas with a Lisa Frank aesthetic because why not.

I'm also quietly building educational content for women who want to enter tech and don't know where to start. No brand, no pressure. Just a message in a bottle left somewhere useful on the internet.

The less glamorous but equally real work right now: sorting accumulated projects and giving them proper READMEs and folder structure. Labeling things for future me — the version who shows up whenever the energy does — is its own kind of skill. Externalizing structure so my brain doesn't have to hold it. It counts.

what i'm learning

Going deeper.

Right now I'm in a machine learning interpretability rabbit hole — specifically SHAP values, which are the kind of thing that make you feel like you finally understand what the model is actually doing versus what it's pretending to do. Also: data engineering fundamentals, home lab infrastructure, and Arduino because I genuinely think electrical engineering is fascinating and I refuse to be stopped.

I learn best when I'm building something a little chaotic that I care about. Structured courses alone put me to sleep.

what i'm reading

Currently on my desk.

I read several things at once and I refuse to apologize for it.

Storytelling with Data — Cole Nussbaumer Knaflic

Learning to find the story before reaching for a chart. Working on chart selection and what I'm calling "upstream thinking" — identifying what actually needs to be said before touching the data.

Modern Data — Kelvin Lin

Getting into the infrastructure side of analytics. Understanding how data actually moves through organizations, not just how to analyze it once it lands.

How to Write Clearly — Tom Albrighton

Because good analysis that nobody can understand is just expensive noise. Clarity is a technical skill and I'm treating it like one.

Somatic Yoga for Nervous System Regulation — LearnWell Books

Same systems thinking as everything else, just pointed inward. Learning to work with my body instead of debugging it from the outside.

All About Love — bell hooks

Just started this one. Already suspicious it's going to rearrange some furniture in my head.

Caught Up — Nessa Allen

Spicy romance on the physical stack. Sometimes a book just needs to be fun and nobody has to justify that.

Wave Song — Emma Hamm audiobook

Fantasy on the commute stack. This is the read-while-doing-other-things slot and it is a sacred slot.

outside the terminal

The rest of it.

A significant piece of my life right now is rebuilding my body with the same patience I'd apply to a complicated dataset. Anti-inflammatory eating, Pilates, somatic yoga, nettle tea — not as a wellness aesthetic but as actual ongoing experiments in what makes this particular nervous system feel like it belongs to me. Slow work. Worth doing.

Outside of that: pottery is on hold but not forgotten. My Stardew Valley farm is an optimization problem I take completely seriously. And I have long-horizon plans for biotope aquariums and plant automation systems, because domestic science is still science and I will not be taking questions.
